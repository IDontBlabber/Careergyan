Why Study in Romania?

Why study in Romania

Romania is becoming an increasingly popular destination for international students seeking quality education at affordable costs. With a rich academic tradition and modern teaching methods, Romanian universities offer a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs—many of which are taught entirely in English. Fields like medicine, engineering, computer science, business, and arts are especially popular among international students.

One of the biggest advantages of studying in Romania is the affordability. Compared to other European countries, both tuition fees and living expenses are significantly lower, making it an ideal choice for students looking for value without compromising on quality. Moreover, Romania is a member of the European Union, so degrees obtained from its universities are recognized across Europe and globally.

Romania offers a multicultural and welcoming environment. Its universities attract students from over 100 countries, providing a vibrant, diverse community. Most institutions have dedicated international student offices to help newcomers adjust and settle in comfortably.

The admission process is simple and straightforward, especially compared to other EU countries. For many programs, entrance exams are not required, and visa approval rates are relatively high for genuine students.

Eligibility

Who can apply to study in Romania

01

Educational Qualifications

The equivalent of Romanian high school (for undergraduate) or a bachelor's degree (for postgraduate) from a recognised institution.

02

Language Proficiency

Proficiency in the language of instruction (Romanian, English or French) through standardised tests or preparatory language courses offered by Romanian universities.

03

Passport and Student Visa

A valid passport and a Romanian student visa (D/SD type), applied for after receiving a letter of acceptance from a recognised Romanian university.

04

Proof of Financial Means

Sufficient funds to cover tuition and living expenses for the duration of studies, usually through bank statements or financial sponsorship.

FAQs

Romania study abroad: common questions

Answers for Indian students planning to study in Romania.

Why should Indian students choose Romania for higher education?
Romania is becoming an increasingly popular destination for international students seeking quality education at affordable costs. Its universities offer many undergraduate and postgraduate programs taught entirely in English, with Medicine, Engineering, Computer Science, Business and Arts especially popular. Tuition fees and living expenses are significantly lower than in many other European countries. As an EU member, Romania's degrees are recognised across Europe and globally.
What are the eligibility requirements to study in Romania?
  • Educational Qualifications: The equivalent of Romanian high school (for undergraduate) or a bachelor's degree (for postgraduate) from a recognised institution.
  • Language Proficiency: Proficiency in the language of instruction (Romanian, English or French) through standardised tests or preparatory language courses offered by Romanian universities.
  • Passport and Student Visa: A valid passport and a Romanian student visa (D/SD type), applied for after receiving a letter of acceptance from a recognised Romanian university.
  • Proof of Financial Means: Sufficient funds to cover tuition and living expenses for the duration of studies, usually through bank statements or financial sponsorship.

Requirements differ by university and course, so your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for your shortlist.

How much does it cost to study in Romania?
  • Tuition fees (undergraduate): £10,000 – £20,000* per year
  • Tuition fees (postgraduate): £12,000 – £25,000* per year
  • Accommodation (university halls): £400 – £800* per month
  • Accommodation (private): £500 – £1,000* per month

* Approximate figures. Actual costs vary by university and city and are subject to change.

What is required for the Romania student visa?
  • Valid passport
  • Romanian student visa (D/SD type), after a letter of acceptance from a recognised Romanian university
  • Proof of sufficient funds for tuition and living expenses

Visa rules and fees change, so we confirm the latest requirements with you before you apply.
