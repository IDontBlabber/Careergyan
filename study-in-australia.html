Eligibility

Who can apply to study in Australia

While the eligibility requirements to study in Australia varies from course to course and college, here is list of the general criteria in brief:

01

Foundation Course

Secondary high school program or other equivalent qualification.

02

Bachelor's Degree

10+2 academic record or equivalent qualification from an affiliated and recognised institution.

03

Master's Degree

Bachelor's degree from a recognised college.

04

English Proficiency

Prove language proficiency with tests such as IELTS, TOEFL or Duolingo. Check which tests your university accepts.

05

Financial Stability

Provide proof of financial resources to show that you can fund your education.

Cost of study

Estimated costs for Indian students

The tuition fee is the most significant cost when studying abroad, covering world-class education, college services like library, student support, and co-curricular activities. Tuition fees are generally high in Australia, especially for international students. The average yearly tuition charges can vary from AUD 20,000 to AUD 50,000* for global learners, and even higher for master’s degree courses or specialised programs. FAQs

Australia study abroad: common questions

Answers for Indian students planning to study in Australia.

Why should Indian students choose Australia for higher education?
Studying in Australia offers numerous opportunities for students worldwide. Its location, scenic views, vibrant environment, culinary scenes and attractive venues all contribute to its popularity. Some of the world's top-ranked universities are in the country, many in the top 100 global universities, such as Melbourne University, Sydney University, UNSW Sydney, Monash University and Queensland University. They offer bachelor's, master's, diploma and PhD courses with remarkable placement opportunities.
What are the eligibility requirements to study in Australia?
  • Foundation Course: Secondary high school program or other equivalent qualification.
  • Bachelor's Degree: 10+2 academic record or equivalent qualification from an affiliated and recognised institution.
  • Master's Degree: Bachelor's degree from a recognised college.
  • English Proficiency: Prove language proficiency with tests such as IELTS, TOEFL or Duolingo. Check which tests your university accepts.

Requirements differ by university and course, so your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for your shortlist.

How much does it cost to study in Australia?
  • Tuition fees (bachelor's degree): AUD 20,000 to AUD 45,000* per year
  • Tuition fees (master's degree and PhD): AUD 22,000 to AUD 50,000* per year
  • Accommodation: AUD 450 to AUD 1,800* per month
  • Food / Groceries: AUD 500 to AUD 1,000* per month

* Approximate figures. Actual costs vary by university and city and are subject to change.

What is required for the Australia student visa?
  • Valid passport and travel documents
  • Letter of acceptance or offer from an Australian university
  • Statement of Purpose showing you are going only for studies and will return home after completion
  • Proof of financial resources to fund your education and living costs in Australia

Visa rules and fees change, so we confirm the latest requirements with you before you apply.

Which documents do I need for university admission in Australia?
A valid passport and identity proof, Letter of Recommendation, English Proficiency Test, Student Sponsor, Latest Education Certificate, 10+2 Qualification Certificate, Proof of Financial Resources, Offer or Acceptance Letter from a Recognised University. You can also download our checklist in the section above.

Our partners include University of Melbourne, University of Sydney, UNSW Sydney, Monash University, University of Queensland and more. We shortlist universities based on your profile, budget and course.
