Why Study in Spain?

Why study in Spain

Spain is a popular European study destination that pairs strong academics with rich culture and a lower cost of living and tuition than many Western European countries. Its universities are noted for business, arts, engineering and humanities, with programs taught in Spanish and English.

Beyond academics, students can enjoy historic cities such as Madrid and Barcelona, Mediterranean beaches and local festivals. A diverse international community and Spain's location make it easy to build global connections and travel across Europe.

Eligibility

Who can apply to study in Spain

01

Academic Qualifications

Undergraduate applicants usually need secondary education equivalent to Spain's Bachillerato, and postgraduate applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree.

02

Language Proficiency

Show Spanish or English proficiency, depending on the course language. Common tests are DELE for Spanish and IELTS or TOEFL for English-taught programs.

03

Student Visa (Non-EU)

Non-EU/EEA students need a student visa, which requires an admission letter, proof of finances, medical insurance and a clean criminal record.

04

Recognition of Qualifications

Some institutions may require previous qualifications to be validated through the Spanish Ministry of Education or the university's admissions office.

FAQs

Spain study abroad: common questions

Answers for Indian students planning to study in Spain.

Why should Indian students choose Spain for higher education?
Spain is a popular European study destination that pairs strong academics with rich culture and a lower cost of living and tuition than many Western European countries. Its universities are noted for business, arts, engineering and humanities, with programs taught in Spanish and English. Beyond academics, students can enjoy historic cities such as Madrid and Barcelona, Mediterranean beaches and local festivals. A diverse international community and Spain's location make it easy to build global connections and travel across Europe.
What are the eligibility requirements to study in Spain?
  • Academic Qualifications: Undergraduate applicants usually need secondary education equivalent to Spain's Bachillerato, and postgraduate applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree.
  • Language Proficiency: Show Spanish or English proficiency, depending on the course language. Common tests are DELE for Spanish and IELTS or TOEFL for English-taught programs.
  • Student Visa (Non-EU): Non-EU/EEA students need a student visa, which requires an admission letter, proof of finances, medical insurance and a clean criminal record.
  • Recognition of Qualifications: Some institutions may require previous qualifications to be validated through the Spanish Ministry of Education or the university's admissions office.

Requirements differ by university and course, so your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for your shortlist.

How much does it cost to study in Spain?
  • Tuition fees (public universities, Bachelor's): €700 – €2,500* per year
  • Accommodation: €300 – €800* per month
  • Food / Groceries: €200 – €400* per month
  • Transportation: €40 – €60* per month (public transport passes)

* Approximate figures. Actual costs vary by university and city and are subject to change.

What is required for the Spain student visa?
  • Letter of acceptance from a Spanish institution
  • Proof of financial means
  • Medical insurance
  • Clean criminal record

Visa rules and fees change, so we confirm the latest requirements with you before you apply.

Which documents do I need for university admission in Spain?
Completed application form, Academic transcripts and certificates, Proof of language proficiency (Spanish or English), Valid passport, Curriculum Vitae (CV), Motivation letter / Statement of Purpose, Letters of recommendation, Recognition of prior studies (Homologación), Proof of financial means, Health insurance coverage. You can also download our checklist in the section above.

Our partners include University of Barcelona, Pompeu Fabra University, Autonomous University of Barcelona (UAB), University of Navarra, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, Complutense University, University of Valencia, Valencia Polytechnic University and more.
