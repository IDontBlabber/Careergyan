Germany Master’s: dMAT and APS

The Digital Master Test (dMAT) is an additional element of APS documentation for selected Master’s applicants from India in affected fields; it is not a medical entrance test. The workshop will explain who should check whether dMAT applies, how to plan preparation and registration, and how dMAT fits into APS and university application timelines. APS verification, university eligibility and the university admission decision remain separate requirements.

✓Understand the dMAT requirement and affected academic areas
✓Preparation approach, mock tests and time management
✓APS document readiness and application sequencing
✓Germany visa awareness, finance and accommodation preparation

APS India has announced dMAT test cycles for 16 January 2027 and 13 March 2027 for relevant applicants. Students should confirm the latest official rules, eligible fields and registration dates before acting.

Italy MBBS: IMAT Preparation and Strategy

The workshop includes a special segment for students considering Medicine in Italy. IMAT is associated with English-taught limited-enrolment programmes in Medicine and Surgery, Dentistry and Veterinary Medicine in Italy. Participants will learn about suitability, eligibility awareness, university selection, non-EU applicant strategy and a preparation framework covering Biology, Chemistry, Physics/Mathematics, logical reasoning and general knowledge.

✓Mock-test practice, speed, accuracy and exam-day strategy
✓University selection, pre-enrolment and financial planning
✓Visa sequencing through the relevant official process
✓Why applicants must wait for the official 2027 IMAT notification
