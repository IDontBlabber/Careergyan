Why Study in Germany?

Why study in Germany

Germany has a rich culture and history that is reflected through its cities. Many beautiful cities are home to old buildings, architecture, and museums, showcasing the past. For those who love art, the country is home to many galleries and museums, which features popular works from artists worldwide. Besides these cultural offerings, Germany is considered one of the best destinations for abroad education. But why? Why study in Germany?

Germany is home to many top-ranked universities, which are manly located in cities with rich cultural heritage. This makes the country an attractive study destination. The highly-ranked colleges offer various academic courses, providing numerous options of what to study and where. Germany is known for its world-class education system and the colleges constantly top the global rankings. As per QS World University Rankings 2024, 9 German colleges are in the list of the top 200 worldwide.

Thus, in addition to being a part of a vibrant, diverse, and thriving culture, you can be at peace knowing that you have come at the right place for achieving academic excellence and career goals.

FAQs

Germany study abroad: common questions

Answers for Indian students planning to study in Germany.

Why should Indian students choose Germany for higher education?
Germany's rich culture and history are reflected in its cities, with old buildings, architecture and museums showing the past. Art lovers can visit many galleries and museums featuring popular works by artists worldwide.
What are the eligibility requirements to study in Germany?
  • For UG Courses: 10+2 qualification from your home country.
  • For PG Courses: A bachelor's degree.
  • Language Proficiency: Prove English proficiency through IELTS or TOEFL. Check requirements with the immigration office and your university.
  • Entrance & Standardised Tests: Some courses require entrance exams or standardised tests. For example, master's programs in business and management may require GRE or GMAT.

Requirements differ by university and course, so your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for your shortlist.

How much does it cost to study in Germany?
  • Tuition fees (public universities): No tuition charges, only administrative charges
  • Tuition fees (private colleges): INR 8 lakhs to INR 17 lakhs*
  • Estimated living expenses: Approximately €1,000* per month, including food, stay, utilities and travel
  • Accommodation: INR 22k to INR 71k* per month

* Approximate figures. Actual costs vary by university and city and are subject to change.

What is required for the Germany student visa?
  • A valid passport
  • €30,000* as health insurance coverage
  • Two recent biometric portrait photographs
  • Filled-in study visa application form
  • Fee payment receipt for the visa application
  • Acceptance letter from a German university
  • A statement of purpose mentioning the intention to return home after course completion
  • Approximately €10,332* deposited in a German fixed deposit account as proof of finances

Visa rules and fees change, so we confirm the latest requirements with you before you apply.

Which documents do I need for university admission in Germany?
German Student Visa, Valid Passport, Language Proficiency Proof, Proof of Financial Resources, Health Insurance, Previous Academic Records, Statement of Purpose, Letters of Recommendation, APS Certificate, Preliminary Review Documentation (VPD), Standardised Exams, University Entrance Qualification. You can also download our checklist in the section above.
What part-time jobs can students do in Germany?
Common student jobs and indicative pay: Student Assistant (€10–€17*), Tutor (€12–€15*), Courier / Delivery (€9–€12*), Waiting Tables (€7–€10*), Office Assistant (€12*). Work rights depend on your visa, so check the limits with your counsellor.
