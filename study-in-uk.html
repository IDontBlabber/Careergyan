Why Study in UK?

Why study in United Kingdom

Arguably there is hardly any destination that is as multicultural and diverse as the UK. Besides being heritage rich, the UK has a large and diverse ethnical diaspora shaped by diverse influences across the world. The UK is not only home to some of the world’s most revered universities but is also home to some of the oldest; synonymous to its heritage preserving character.By why is UK a compelling prospect as your study-abroad destination?

Academic programs in the UK are in fact quite affordable as they are also characterised with short-term degree programs which may not be found elsewhere. From research-based programs to choosing the course of their choice; students are exposed to a multitude of graduate and under-graduate programs that are embedded with diverse educational curriculums. The programs also offer students the flexibility to pick and combine various courses and merge them to their under-graduate program. For example, you can combine a non-conventional course with a traditional program, or with any interdisciplinary course and clinch joint honours, bespoke degrees, to accentuate your prospects for employment.

So, besides being part of a diverse and thriving community, and a culturally diverse environment, you can rest assured that studying in the UK is a pursuance to achieve the highest degree of academic excellence.

Eligibility

Who can apply to study in UK

While eligibility to study in the UK primarily depends on your age and academic qualification, factors such as means of sponsorship, economic stability, language proficiency, are all decisive. Below are your guidelines in brief -

01

Foundation

Higher Secondary (12th grade) or equivalent.

02

Undergraduate

Completed secondary school or 12th grade.

03

Postgraduate

A bachelor's degree in any field. Some programs require an upper second-class honours degree; others accept a lower second with professional work experience.

04

English

Secure English Language Tests (SELT) are accepted. IELTS is recognised by UK Visas and Immigration. TOEFL is not currently a recognised SELT, though some universities may accept it, so check with your university.

05

Entrance tests

Some programs require tests such as GMAT, BMAT, GRE, SAT or MAT, in addition to English proficiency tests.

FAQs

UK study abroad: common questions

Answers for Indian students planning to study in United Kingdom.

Why should Indian students choose United Kingdom for higher education?
The UK is multicultural and diverse, and home to some of the world's oldest and most respected universities. Programs can be more affordable and sometimes shorter than in other countries.
What are the eligibility requirements to study in UK?
  • Foundation: Higher Secondary (12th grade) or equivalent.
  • Undergraduate: Completed secondary school or 12th grade.
  • Postgraduate: A bachelor's degree in any field. Some programs require an upper second-class honours degree; others accept a lower second with professional work experience.
  • English: Secure English Language Tests (SELT) are accepted. IELTS is recognised by UK Visas and Immigration. TOEFL is not currently a recognised SELT, though some universities may accept it, so check with your university.

Requirements differ by university and course, so your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for your shortlist.

How much does it cost to study in UK?
  • Tuition, public universities: £9,000–£20,000* (UG) · £10,000–£28,000* (PG) per year
  • Tuition, private universities: £10,000–£40,000* (UG) · £15,000–£35,000* (PG) per year
  • Student halls: £650–£2,000* per year, plus about one month's rent as deposit
  • Private accommodation: £350–£650/month* shared · £800–£1,300/month* private

* Approximate figures. Actual costs vary by university and city and are subject to change.

What is required for the UK student visa?
  • Valid passport
  • Evidence of funds
  • Tuberculosis screening (compulsory for South Asian countries)
  • CAS reference from your institution
  • Evidence that the visa is genuinely for study

Visa rules and fees change, so we confirm the latest requirements with you before you apply.

Which documents do I need for university admission in UK?
Passport and ID, Licensed student sponsor, CAS letter, Offer letter, Financial statement, English proficiency letter, Criminal record (DBS) check, Three recommendation letters, 10+2 certificate, Latest qualification certificate, ATAS certificate, SELT certificate (IELTS). You can also download our checklist in the section above.

Our partners include University of East London, University of Sheffield, Queen Mary University of London, University of Birmingham, University of Roehampton, Coventry University, University of Essex, Aston University and more. We shortlist universities based on your profile, budget and course.
