Why Study in Poland?

Why study in Poland

Poland has emerged as a popular study destination for international students due to its excellent academic standards, affordable tuition fees, and vibrant student life. Polish universities offer a wide range of English-taught programs in fields like engineering, medicine, business, and IT, with degrees recognized across the European Union and globally. The country boasts a strong academic tradition, modern infrastructure, and an emphasis on practical, career-focused learning. Students benefit from high-quality education while enjoying a lower cost of living compared to many Western European countries.

In addition to academics, Poland provides a culturally enriching experience with its blend of historic cities, lively student communities, and a welcoming atmosphere for international learners. With a central location in Europe, students can easily travel to neighboring countries during breaks. Moreover, Poland’s growing economy offers post-study work opportunities, especially in sectors such as technology, healthcare, and manufacturing. Supportive visa policies and scholarships further make Poland an attractive and accessible option for students from around the world.

Eligibility

Who can apply to study in Poland

To study in Poland, international students must meet academic, language, and financial requirements. A valid student visa and acceptance from a recognized university are essential. Additionally, students need to prove financial stability for tuition fees and living expenses.

01

Academic Qualifications

Secondary education or higher, depending on the level of the course.

02

English Proficiency

Non-native speakers must provide proof of English proficiency, such as IELTS, TOEFL or another accepted test.

03

Offer of Place

A formal acceptance letter from a recognised Polish institution is needed to apply for a student visa.

04

Financial Support

Show enough funds to cover tuition, living costs and travel for the full length of your studies.

FAQs

Poland study abroad: common questions

Answers for Indian students planning to study in Poland.

Why should Indian students choose Poland for higher education?
Poland is a popular choice for international students because of its strong academics, reasonable tuition and lively student life. Many English-taught programs in engineering, medicine, business and IT are recognised across the EU and globally, and living costs are lower than in many Western European countries. Beyond academics, Poland offers historic cities, active student communities and a welcoming atmosphere. Its central location makes it easy to travel to neighbouring countries, and the growing economy creates post-study work options in technology, healthcare and manufacturing.
What are the eligibility requirements to study in Poland?
  • Academic Qualifications: Secondary education or higher, depending on the level of the course.
  • English Proficiency: Non-native speakers must provide proof of English proficiency, such as IELTS, TOEFL or another accepted test.
  • Offer of Place: A formal acceptance letter from a recognised Polish institution is needed to apply for a student visa.
  • Financial Support: Show enough funds to cover tuition, living costs and travel for the full length of your studies.

Requirements differ by university and course, so your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for your shortlist.

How much does it cost to study in Poland?
  • Tuition fees: EUR 2,000–EUR 4,000* per year (approx. ₹1.8–₹3.5* lakhs*)
  • Accommodation: EUR 150–EUR 400* per month (approx. ₹13,000–₹35,000*)
  • Food and groceries: EUR 150–EUR 250* per month (approx. ₹13,000–₹22,000*)
  • Health insurance: EUR 50–EUR 100* per year (approx. ₹4,500–₹9,000*)

* Approximate figures. Actual costs vary by university and city and are subject to change.

What is required for the Poland student visa?
  • A valid student visa
  • Acceptance from a recognised university
  • An official offer letter from a Polish education provider
  • Proof of financial stability for tuition fees and living expenses

Visa rules and fees change, so we confirm the latest requirements with you before you apply.
