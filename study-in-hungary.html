Why Study in Hungary?

Why study in Hungary

Situated in Central/Eastern Europe, bordering Austria in the West and Romania in the East, Hungary has many high-class colleges. Do you know what makes study in Hungary famous?

Diverse programs are taught in English in top-class Hungarian universities. From data science and cyber security to architecture and social sciences, numerous are available for you. The institutions even offer preparatory and foundational courses to those who cannot pass the entrance exams. The degrees offered in Hungary follow the European Bologna Scheme and are recognised throughout Europe and the world.

So, by studying in Hungary, you can be at peace knowing you are receiving a high-quality education with a widely recognised degree and numerous opportunities.

Eligibility

Who can apply to study in Hungary

The eligibility requirements required to study in Hungary are as follows:

01

Language Proficiency

English-taught courses require proof of English proficiency through IELTS, TOEFL or an official language certificate. Courses taught in Hungarian require proficiency in Hungarian.

02

Academic Records

Bachelor's degree courses require completion of 12th grade or equivalent. Postgraduate programs require an undergraduate program in the same field.

03

Standardised Exams

Certain STEM courses require an entrance exam covering subjects such as English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or Biology. Students who cannot pass take a foundation or preparatory course.

FAQs

Hungary study abroad: common questions

Answers for Indian students planning to study in Hungary.

Why should Indian students choose Hungary for higher education?
Hungary sits in Central/Eastern Europe, bordering Austria to the west and Romania to the east, and has many high-class colleges. Top Hungarian universities teach diverse programs in English, including data science, cyber security, architecture and social sciences. Institutions also offer preparatory and foundation courses for students who do not pass entrance exams. Hungarian degrees follow the Bologna Scheme and are recognised across Europe and worldwide.
What are the eligibility requirements to study in Hungary?
  • Language Proficiency: English-taught courses require proof of English proficiency through IELTS, TOEFL or an official language certificate. Courses taught in Hungarian require proficiency in Hungarian.
  • Academic Records: Bachelor's degree courses require completion of 12th grade or equivalent. Postgraduate programs require an undergraduate program in the same field.
  • Standardised Exams: Certain STEM courses require an entrance exam covering subjects such as English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or Biology. Students who cannot pass take a foundation or preparatory course.

Requirements differ by university and course, so your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for your shortlist.

How much does it cost to study in Hungary?
  • Bachelor's programs: €2,000 – €5,000* per year (public) · €5,000 – €12,000* (private)
  • Master's programs: €3,000 – €6,000* per year (public) · €6,000 – €15,000* (private)
  • Medical degrees (MBBS): €12,000 – €16,000* (public) · €16,000 – €20,000* (private)
  • Doctoral programs: €1,500 – €5,000* (public) · €5,000 – €12,000* per year (private)

* Approximate figures. Actual costs vary by university and city and are subject to change.

What is required for the Hungary student visa?
  • Letter of acceptance from a recognised Hungarian college
  • Proof of financial resources to cover tuition fees and living costs
  • Valid passport and proof of identity
  • Health insurance and a medical certificate

Visa rules and fees change, so we confirm the latest requirements with you before you apply.

Which documents do I need for university admission in Hungary?
Passport and Identity Proof, Academic Records, Proof of language proficiency, Statement of Purpose, Letter of Recommendation, Residence Permit, Proof of Accommodation, Health insurance, Financial proof to cover your stay. You can also download our checklist in the section above.
